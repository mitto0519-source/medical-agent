Children and adolescents with diabetes and their parents and caregivers should receive culturally sensitive and developmentally appropriate individualized diabetes self-management education and support (DSMES) according to national standards at diagnosis and routinely thereafter. Recommendations for managing type 1 diabetes are comprehensively addressed in the complete American Diabetes Association (ADA) Standards of Care in Diabetes—2025. School and Child Care Refer to the ADA position statements “Diabetes Care in the School Setting” and “Care of Young Children With Diabetes in the Childcare and Community Setting” and the ADA’s Safe at School website ( https://diabetes.org/advocacy/safe-at-school-state-laws/position-statements ) for additional details.
