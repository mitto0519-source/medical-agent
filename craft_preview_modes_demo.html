Smoking status and incident cardiovascular disease among Korean adults: a prospective cohort study

KNHANES-linked cohort · N = 18,420 · 94,300 person-years

Results

In this cohort study of 18,420 Korean adults (mean age, 47.2 years) with 94,300 person-years of follow-up, current smoking was associated with a higher risk of incident cardiovascular disease (adjusted HR, 1.74; 95% CI, 1.41–2.15) compared with never smoking.

A dose-response relationship was observed across smoking-quantity tertiles (P for trend < .001). The association was stronger among men than women (P for interaction = .03).
